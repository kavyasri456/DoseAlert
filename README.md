# DoseAlert
## Description
DoseAlert is a smart medication reminder system that ensures patients take their medicines on time and automatically alerts caregivers if a dose is missed
## Problem Statement
 Elderly patients often forgot to take medicines on time and that missed does can lead to serious health complications because the caregivers of them usually find out too late.
 ## Problem Solution
 DoseALert sends timely medicine remainders to elderly users and acquires a simple confirmation of taken or missed. It automatically alerts caregiver if a dose is missed or unconfirmed.
 ## Features
 -> Medicine schedule setup
 -> Timely remainder notifications
 -> One tap taken/Missed confirmation
 -> Automatic Caregiver alert on Missed or Unconfirmed doses
 -> Simple and elderly friendly interface
 -> Prescription digitalization
 -> Emergency contact listing
 ## How To Run
 1. firstly enter the patient email id and password.
 2. Now enter caregiver's name, email and phone number.
 3. you've been logged in.
 4. Enter medication name and the time of the medication
 5. now click on Add remainder.
 6. You can see your prescription details in the portal "Your remainders".
 7. When the clock tik's you will get a alarm remainder displaying teken or missed buttons.
 8. Once the time limits of 5 minutes exceeds then it will automatically masks as missed.
 9. and give a popup message of email has sent to caregiver.
